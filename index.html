How to use

How to import into Scratch:

  1. Upload your .obj and .mtl files above.
  2. Click the Download TXT button once it becomes green.
  3. Import the generated text file into a project's list.

Troubleshooting:

  • Triangles only: Ensure your 3D software (like Blender) exports the model with the "Triangulate Faces" option checked.
